# Cyberpunk 2099: The Neon Heist 🏙️⚡

An interactive, high-stakes cyberpunk text adventure built for browser play and hosted on **GitHub Pages**.

## 🕹️ Story Overview
Set in **Neo-Shinjuku Sector 9**, you play as an elite netrunner infiltrating the 80th-floor vault of Arasaka-Kuro Corp to steal the **50 Million Credit Quantum AI Chip**.

## ⚙️ Features
* **Cyberware & Heat Tracking:** Monitor your live Heat levels, Cyberware energy, and acquired Credit bounties.
* **Neon Aesthetic:** Retro cyber-terminal UI featuring cyan/magenta glowing panels and ASCII art.
* **Multiple Escape Routes:** Choose your heist methods and escape via flying AV-4 vehicles, holo-chute dives, or elevator cable hacks.
